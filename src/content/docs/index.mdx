## From regulatory requirements to operational control

French HSE requirements can be complex to interpret and implement at site level. SASERI turns regulatory obligations and management-system requirements into clear, auditable and practical measures adapted to your activities, organisation and risk profile.

<div className="domains grid gap-x-12 md:grid-cols-2">
<section>

### Audit & Compliance

HSE and QSE audits, compliance assessments and site reviews to identify gaps, prioritise corrective actions and provide corporate and local teams with a clear view of their compliance status.

</section>
<section>

### Legal Register

Site-specific HSE legal registers identifying the French regulatory requirements applicable to your activities and providing a structured basis for compliance assessment, action planning and follow-up.

</section>
<section>

### DUERP & Risk Assessment

Preparation and review of the French Document Unique d'Évaluation des Risques Professionnels (DUERP), combining regulatory compliance with a practical assessment of occupational risks, existing controls and prevention measures.

</section>
<section>

### Management Systems

Design, implementation and improvement of ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 management systems, including integrated systems aligned with corporate requirements and local operations.

</section>
